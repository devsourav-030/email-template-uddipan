CEPT Chronicles

The student-run newspaper of CEPT University, Ahmedabad. Editorially independent since 1979. Published every Thursday during term — in print and online.

We cover campus news, urban issues, arts, research, and student life. Every voice on campus is welcome — whether you write, photograph, or have a story to share.
